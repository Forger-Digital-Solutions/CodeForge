## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited activities when using the CodeForge software application and associated cloud services. This AUP applies to all users, organizations, and automated agents interacting with CodeForge.

## 2. Prohibited Uses of Autonomous Agents & Tool Execution
You agree not to direct, configure, or prompt CodeForge agents to:
- **Malware & Cyberattacks**: Develop, compile, test, or distribute malicious software, viruses, rootkits, spyware, ransomware, keyloggers, or tools designed to exploit security vulnerabilities without authorization.
- **Unauthorized System Access**: Scan, probe, or attempt to gain unauthorized access to remote systems, servers, networks, or databases.
- **Denial of Service**: Launch denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, flood networks, or exhaust computational resources of third parties.
- **Deceptive Autonomous Behavior**: Impersonate human developers in open-source projects or code review queues without disclosing AI involvement where required by community guidelines.

## 3. Provider Abuse & Multi-Accounting
When utilizing CodeForge routing or ForgeZero capabilities:
- You may not create multiple accounts, spoof IP addresses, or deploy automated account-generation scripts to circumvent rate limits or free-tier quotas on CodeForge Cloud or any upstream provider (e.g., OpenRouter, Google AI Studio, Groq).
- You may not resell raw API access or operate a commercial multi-tenant proxy service powered by CodeForge without prior written authorization from Forger Digital Solutions and applicable providers.

## 4. Unlawful & Regulated Content
You may not use CodeForge to generate, process, or transmit:
- Content that infringes on third-party intellectual property rights, trademarks, or copyrights.
- Unlawfully obtained trade secrets or proprietary code in violation of an active non-disclosure agreement (NDA).
- Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA, or non-public financial records, unless appropriate compliance agreements are executed.
- Content that promotes violence, terrorism, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or illegal discrimination.
