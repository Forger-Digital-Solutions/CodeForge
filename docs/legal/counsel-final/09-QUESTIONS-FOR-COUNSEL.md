# Questions for Counsel

1. What exact Terms and Privacy disclosures are required for a Windows BYOK
   beta whose prompts and source context may be sent to user-selected providers?
2. How should provider-specific retention, human review, and training practices
   be disclosed when they vary by provider, model, tier, and region?
3. Is the current host-command disclosure sufficient for commands executed with
   the user's OS permissions and configurable approval settings?
4. What age-policy wording and jurisdictional handling should accompany the
   owner-authorized 18+ acknowledgement?
5. What exact MIT copyright holder should be placed in the root license?
6. What warranty, liability, indemnity, and acceptable-use language is required?
7. What Windows signing, SmartScreen, trademark, and distribution notices are
   required before public download?
8. What approved support/contact route and deletion language should accompany
   the beta?
