# Product and Launch Scope

## In scope

- Windows 10/11 x64 desktop application.
- User-owned OpenCode Zen and OpenRouter credentials where supported.
- Local project work, model selection, approvals, cancellation, and recovery.
- ForgeZero free-model eligibility and fail-closed routing.

## Out of scope

- Public release or public download activation.
- Hosted shared-key routing, paid CodeForge subscriptions, Stripe, GEMS, and
  enterprise offerings.
- Provider agreements accepted on behalf of users.
- Legal terms publication or attorney certification.

The beta release notes are the user-facing scope summary:
`docs/releases/codeforge-desktop-byok-beta-r1.md`.
