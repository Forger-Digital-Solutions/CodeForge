# Proposed Legal Documents

No Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, provider agreement, or other legal
document is activated or published by this milestone. The beta release notes
are technical/product scope copy only and must not be treated as contractual
terms.

Counsel should provide or approve the documents required for the intended
distribution, including provider data-use disclosures, host-command/autonomy
disclosures, age/minor language, warranty/liability, governing law, and
support/contact language.
