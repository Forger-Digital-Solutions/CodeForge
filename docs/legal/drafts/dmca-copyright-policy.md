## 2. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent thereof and believe that material hosted on CodeForge Cloud infringes your copyright, you may submit a written notice containing the following statutory information (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)):
1. **Identification of the Work**: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed;
2. **Identification of Infringing Material**: Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple works at a single online site are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works;
3. **Location of Material**: Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity and information reasonably sufficient to permit CodeForge to locate the material (e.g., URL or unique session/publication ID);
4. **Contact Details**: Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, such as an address, telephone number, and email address;
5. **Good Faith Statement**: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
6. **Accuracy Statement**: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Send the written notice to: `dmca@codeforge.dev`.

## 3. DMCA Counter-Notification Procedure

If your hosted content was removed or disabled as a result of a DMCA takedown notice and you believe the removal was due to a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notification containing (17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3)):
1. Your physical or electronic signature;
2. Identification of the material that has been removed or disabled and the location at which the material appeared before removal;
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification;
4. Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or [BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED: Judicial District] if outside the United States), and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original takedown notice.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, CodeForge will forward it to the original complaining party. If the copyright owner does not file a court action seeking a restraining order within ten (10) to fourteen (14) business days, we may restore the removed material.

In accordance with 17 U.S.C. § 512(i)(1)(A), CodeForge maintains a policy that provides for the immediate suspension or permanent termination, in appropriate circumstances, of cloud accounts and hosting privileges of users who are determined to be repeat copyright infringers.
