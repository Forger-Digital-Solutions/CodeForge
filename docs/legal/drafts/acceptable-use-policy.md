This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs the use of CodeForge software, services, interfaces, and connected artificial intelligence models provided by [BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED: Forger Digital Solutions Operating Entity Name] ("CodeForge," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy incorporates and flows down mandatory restrictions imposed by our upstream model providers and infrastructure partners.

By accessing or using CodeForge, you agree to comply strictly with this AUP. Violation of this policy may result in immediate suspension or termination of your account, revocation of cloud access, and appropriate legal action.

---

## 1. Prohibited Cyber & Security Activities

You may NOT use CodeForge or its autonomous agent tooling to:
1. **Malicious Software**: Develop, test, compile, obfuscate, or distribute malware, ransomware, spyware, trojans, rootkits, or botnets.
2. **Exploits & Cyberattacks**: Generate zero-day exploits, craft automated denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) payloads, or execute unauthorized network vulnerability scanning against systems without explicit, written authorization from the system owner.
3. **Credential Harvester Tools**: Develop credential dumpers, phishing kits, keyloggers, or unauthorized authentication bypass software.
4. **Circumvention of Access Controls**: Attempt to reverse engineer, bypass, or tamper with CodeForge's ForgeZero firewall, rate limits, credit ledger, or provider quota tracking mechanisms.

---

## 2. Prohibited AI & Model Usage (Upstream Flow-Down)

You must strictly adhere to the policies of connected third-party model providers (OpenRouter, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta). You may NOT use CodeForge to:
1. **Foundational Model Distillation**: Use completions, embeddings, or synthetic outputs generated by OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google models to train, evaluate, or fine-tune competing frontier foundational language models.
2. **Allowance & Rate Limit Abuse**: Utilize multi-account pooling, rotating proxies, or automated bot farms to circumvent free tier quota limits, rate limits, or allowance policies on OpenRouter, Google, or Groq.
3. **Resale of Raw Inference**: Resell, re-license, or provide unauthorized multi-tenant proxy access to third-party model APIs in violation of Section 7(4) of the OpenRouter Terms of Service or equivalent provider restrictions.
4. **Disinformation & Deceptive Content**: Generate deepfakes, automated political influence campaigns, fraudulent legal or medical advice, or deceptive synthetic materials without prominent disclosure.

---

## 3. High-Risk System Restrictions

CodeForge and its autonomous code generation features are NOT fault-tolerant and are NOT engineered or certified for use in mission-critical, high-risk, or hazardous environments. You may NOT deploy CodeForge autonomous execution loops in connection with:
- Operation of nuclear facilities or power grids;
- Aircraft navigation, air traffic control, or commercial flight control systems;
- Life support systems, medical implant management, or intensive care monitoring;
- Autonomous weapons systems or critical national defense infrastructure.

---

## 4. Autonomous Agent Execution Responsibilities

CodeForge provides autonomous tool dispatch ("Full-Auto" and "8-Bit" modes) that executes shell and file commands on your host workstation. Under this policy, you agree:
1. Not to launch autonomous loops with administrative/root privileges (`sudo` or elevated PowerShell) unless strictly necessary for a bounded developer task;
2. Not to direct the agent to delete, repartition, or modify system-critical operating system directories;
3. To maintain active monitoring and retain recent git checkpoints prior to initiating complex multi-turn refactoring loops.
