## CodeForge eligibility evidence gap

The repository [README](../../../README.md) establishes a public product, source, and release path. It does **not** establish legal incorporation, company age, funding status, recent funding date, business-domain email, billing-account ownership, prior awards, or program acceptance. Those facts cannot be inferred from the GitHub organization or product name. Therefore no program is labeled “eligible” or “realistically secured” here. The initial tiers above are candidates conditional on the owner confirming facts and program review.

## Billing and credit-safety findings

- [Cloudflare's program](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/) lasts one year; a $350k headline includes at most $50k Workers AI credits, and the entry tier includes at most $2.5k Workers AI. A startup award must be tied to a specific account and its allowed services before the broker can count it. Workers Paid can bill usage beyond the daily included amount; never let its status alone satisfy ForgeZero.
- [AWS promotional credits](https://aws.amazon.com/awscredits/) require a credit card; excluded services and spend past available credit can bill. [AWS Budgets](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/budgets-managing-costs.html) updates up to three times a day, typically 8–12 hours apart, and charge notifications can lag. IAM/service quotas and CodeForge's own token-price admission are required before traffic.
- [Google's alerts-only budgets](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/budgets) do not cap spend. [Preview spend-cap budgets](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/budgets-spend-caps) can pause Gemini API and Agent Platform usage for a selected project/service, but enforcement has reporting latency and in-flight requests can bill. The cap must sit below remaining credits with an independent local stop.
- [Microsoft's FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/other) says a credit card is required and Azure subscriptions transition to pay-as-you-go when credit is consumed. [Foundry sponsorship](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/benefits/technical-benefits/azure-credits/foundry-model-sponsorship-coverage) excludes third-party and Marketplace-billed models. Enrollment is a billing decision, not merely a free-credit claim.
- [Modal's net spend limit](https://modal.com/docs/guide/budgets) stops workloads that would incur out-of-pocket usage when reached, but its separate workspace budget and storage/reservation charge scopes need checking in the actual account before treating it as a hard zero-overage guard.

These cloud credits are promotional resources with potential billable overage. For ForgeAuto/Free, credit-funded routes should be eligible only after an award receipt, exact SKU coverage, remaining-balance telemetry, commercial and data-use clearance, and an enforced no-unauthorized-spend control are recorded. Uncertain terms or balance must fail closed. A credit balance does not multiply per user and has no concurrency guarantee by itself.

## Acquisition sequence

1. Establish the legal and account facts in the [application package](application-package.md). Record private proof outside Git; commit only redacted status and evidence hashes.
2. Ask Cloudflare to resolve the Tier 3 self-funded/funding-recency wording and identify precise Workers AI credit mechanics and an account-level overage stop. A tier decision would turn the entry offer into a bounded, service-specific candidate.
3. Evaluate AWS Founders and Google Start against current ownership, prior-award, and billing records. Obtain written eligible-service/SKU coverage for intended inference rather than assuming all cloud credits apply to every model.
4. Evaluate Microsoft only after pay-as-you-go exposure and Foundry model billing source have an enforceable control plan.
5. Pursue higher tiers only with genuine qualifying funding or provider affiliations. Do not acquire Org IDs through unrelated organizations, rotate accounts, or represent speculative amounts as available capacity.
6. After a legitimate award, store a dated grant receipt, expiration, covered product list, AI subcap, remaining balance, per-SKU price, observed quota domain and concurrency, and a stopped-at-credit-boundary test. Then and only then promote the pool from opportunity to broker supply.
