Production changes address three concrete role-intelligence defects. A fresh role receipt now re-enters the governed qualification queue after three distinct current negative run outcomes for that same role; one failure, repeated evidence for one run, unrelated role failures, and capacity events cannot trigger it. Role probes now stop after the first upstream transient, leave later roles NOT_TESTED, and mark the receipt retryable. This avoids both wasted free quota and false partial promotion. Free Fabric now bounds Reviewer pool independence by qualification tier and role score, so a weak independent candidate cannot displace a substantially better qualified Reviewer. The Reviewer instruction now asks for a verdict once the supplied diff resolves the task; the structured verdict contract and blocker rules remain intact.

The actual Free Fabric replay tests and 34-decision long-horizon simulation show bounded negative winner flips, positive recovery, role isolation, expiry, and no capacity-quality contamination. Runtime quality remains advisory; it cannot grant a role against a failed qualification floor. The failed checkout Coder received negative role evidence but still won its later live admission because no better qualified route was concurrently available. There is no hard-coded Nemotron blacklist or paid spillover.
