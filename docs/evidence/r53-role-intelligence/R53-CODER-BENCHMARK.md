# R53 Coder benchmark

The live production compact suite in `R53-ROLE-BENCHMARK.json` exercised two Coder cases with generated patches and tool calls. Nemotron Super passed 2/2 before a later provider interruption made the full role-aware receipt inconclusive; Groq GPT-OSS 20B and Mistral Codestral 2508 each passed 2/2. The compact cases are narrow capability checks and do not establish multi-file reliability on their own.

The autonomous receipts supply stronger task evidence. `R53-LIVE-MISSION-MULTI-STEP.json` required fixing multiply, formatting, and median behavior in three source files; Groq GPT-OSS 120B completed the Coder role in 14 requests and 13 tools, with four passing integration tests. `R53-LIVE-MISSION-FEATURE.json` exposed a genuine Coder failure: Nemotron Lightning attempted the absolute `/workspace` path, the runtime blocked it as `TOOL_WORKSPACE_ESCAPE`, no edits integrated, and five checks still failed. `R53-LIVE-MISSION-FEATURE-RECOVERY.json` replayed that role-specific failure into production ranking; under the actual concurrent capacity and policy constraints Lightning remained the only admitted Coder when its child started, then completed the checkout feature in 17 requests and 20 tools. The independent test command passed 9/9, ForgeVerify completed, and integrated/verified tree hashes matched.

The recovery is a real completion after a recorded negative role signal, but it is not evidence that one signal demoted Lightning below an available peer. Runtime score fell to 56 with `ROLE_RUNTIME_NEGATIVE`, while the concurrent route constraints still selected it. The deterministic production Free Fabric replay in `packages/eight-bit/test/r50-quality-routing-replay.test.ts` proves winner flips after repeated failures and after verified recovery. No qualification status was changed from these mission outcomes alone.

The separate `R53-LIVE-MISSION-REFACTOR.json` attempted a shared aggregation refactor. After a Groq Qwen 429, Nemotron Super used the remaining 26 model turns and 29 tools without completing; the post-run probe still lacked `sum` and `summarize`. `R53-LIVE-MISSION-REFACTOR-RECOVERY.json` replayed that one witnessed non-convergence into role selection. Nemotron Lightning then used 25 requests and 31 tools, reached the turn limit, and left the same exports missing. Neither run reached ForgeVerify. These are direct negative multi-file Coder results, while the 429 is capacity-only. The changed route cannot be credited solely to the replay because capacity conditions differed.

`R53-LIVE-MISSION-REFACTOR-DIAGNOSTIC.json` used Groq GPT-OSS 120B on the same fixture with a 600-second harness bound. It was cancelled after 19 requests and 18 distinct-argument tools, including two completed edits. The tests still failed; this is an incomplete run rather than a role-quality failure or duplicate-loop proof. Its free-route eligibility and zero paid spend are recorded in the receipt.

Coverage remains incomplete for a verified live refactor and a cross-package failing integration task. The role suite has no six-category Coder benchmark, so these results must not be presented as such.
