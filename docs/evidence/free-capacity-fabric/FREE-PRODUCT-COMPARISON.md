| Product | Quota owner / access | CodeForge decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kilo Gateway Auto Free](https://kilo.ai/docs/gateway/authentication) | Anonymous free requests: public IP, documented 200/hour/IP. | [GitHub Copilot SDK](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/copilot-sdk/features/usage-and-billing) | Official SDK exposes account quota and model credit pricing for the authenticated user's entitlement. | Sanctioned user-entitlement candidate. The existing registry remains gated until account-specific Free eligibility, overage hard stop, privacy and managed relay terms are established. No new integration is claimed. |
| [Cline public API](https://github.com/cline/cline/blob/main/docs/api/getting-started.mdx) | Official OpenAI-compatible API requires a Cline account and API key; its guide includes a no-credit Free-model example. | Legitimate user-account or sponsor candidate, requiring separate quota, terms, privacy and no-paid-fallback verification. No CodeForge account access or admitted route is claimed. |
| [OpenRouter Free](https://openrouter.ai/blog/tutorials/how-to-get-the-lowest-cost-llm-inference-on-openrouter/) | Account-level daily and minute limits, shared across the account's free models. | Existing shared key remains supplemental; model names do not create independent quota domains. |

These references establish quota placement and product patterns. They do not admit a CodeForge route without current terms, privacy, cost, qualification, and runtime receipts.
