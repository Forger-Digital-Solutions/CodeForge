## Result

SWE-2 is the newest Cognition SWE release found. It may be useful as a product-level reference and possibly an external-agent evaluation subject later, but it does **not** qualify as a direct 8-Bit/16-Bit model integration on the evidence currently available. No SWE-family paid or free request has been made. This keeps capability claims, Devin product access, and raw inference eligibility separate.
