# R71 free supply research — 2026-10-08

**27 provider routes investigated. One newly verified personal free account: OpenRouter.** This account passed the actual FreeCloudService → FreeFabric → AgentRuntime path and performed a real file edit. Kilo remains the packaged anonymous route. Its quota is per public IP, including authenticated requests; it is not independently multiplied by users behind one NAT. No catalog counts below are counted as usable coding supply.

This matrix is a dated research admission decision, not an unconditional production allowlist. QUALIFIED applies only to the named measured role, account, consent, current policy and exact-zero model. PROBATION means plausible official integration with incomplete evidence. BLOCKED identifies a concrete missing prerequisite. REJECTED means incompatible free economics, authorization or production use. UNKNOWN means the necessary primary evidence was unavailable. Every unverified field stays unknown; no inferred quota or privacy promise is used for admission.

## Integration priorities and economics

OpenRouter is the actionable verified addition using the existing official product connection and owner-scoped production fabric. SambaNova and exact-zero Z.ai Flash are the next promising account-key candidates; neither is admitted without an actual account and current entitlement/legal/privacy/tool receipts. Gemini, Mistral and Workers AI require billing-disabled account or project proof. Puter and Copilot SDK require a different official adapter plus provable free-only plan accounting. OAuth support alone is not enough.

For N genuinely distinct user-owned unpaid OpenRouter accounts, the documented ceiling is 50N requests/day, subject to each account's 20 RPM and shared upstream availability. For P genuinely distinct public-IP Kilo domains, the ceiling is 200P requests/hour, not 200 times registered users. Multiple users behind NAT share Kilo. These ceilings cannot be converted to completed coding tasks without measuring task cost and quality; the campaign needed 24 and 32 model requests for two noncompletions. OpenRouter and Kilo can also share an upstream model provider, so account independence does not guarantee independent upstream availability.

No operator-funded inference, purchased subscriptions, local LLMs, account farming, IP rotation, paid fallback, GEMS Auto or Paid Auto were added. No artificial registered-user cap was introduced. Research did not automatically change the provider allowlist or interpret stale registry descriptions as live entitlement.
