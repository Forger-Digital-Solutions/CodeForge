# R73 reliability

The authoritative machine-readable task metrics are `reliability-summary.json`, regenerated by `benchmarks/r73/summarize-evidence.mjs` from every retained live campaign. Qualification cases and provider/catalog probes are reported separately from autonomous task attempts. Parent-level completion, required review/ForgeVerify/integration and tests-after are cross-checked; false completion remains a failing invariant.

The first two attempts completed zero parents. The first had correct editing/testing followed by Coder turn exhaustion; the second completed its Coder then failed Reviewer output/tool continuation. Neither failure is charged as a provider outage. The later recovery and streak results must be included in the final summary before a completion percentage is reported.

R72's baseline was zero completed / one attempted. Its Reviewer issued zero requests, despite the historical telemetry incorrectly marking one provider failure. R73 keeps that historical receipt and corrects new worker telemetry to observed provider request failures, zero for zero-request admission and nullable unknown coverage where request observations are absent. The operational dataset skips unknown provider labels.

The runtime's duplicate-work counter is not a count of every repeated read. The first attempt's repeated file/git exploration and watch-mode stall are independently observable in raw tools; no duplicate source edit is claimed. Recovery preserves hashes, tests, changed files and constraints, while finite budgets and honest blocked states remain enforced.

Twenty real attempts are a conditional endurance target. Scarce quota, incomplete Reviewer protocol compatibility or unavailable independent capacity is a reason to stop and repair/preserve evidence, not to manufacture a streak or deliberately exhaust a provider.
