# R73 addendum: account routing validation

The addendum tests exercise production connection guards, ForgeZero, FreeFabric reservations, model execution and the autonomous role runtime with isolated fixture transports. Fixture inference is deterministic and incurs no external charge. It is not live-provider qualification or parent-level autonomous completion evidence.

## Production path coverage

`packages/server/test/personal-free-capacity.test.ts` covers owner-bound admission, disclosure consent, actual role suitability, qualification freshness, authorization freshness, distinct physical quota identities, minimum remaining allowances across connection aliases, preservation of additional upstream restriction windows, and reservations shared by legitimate owners of the same upstream quota domain.

`packages/server/test/personal-account-execution.test.ts` covers the production model-execution stamp reaching the durable spending guard, unique physical attempt identities, absence of internal account metadata in upstream request bodies, zero POSTs under Free Only or missing consent/budget authority, foreign-owner refusal, revocation isolation, incompatible role refusal, and personal Free execution requiring both ForgeZero registration and FreeFabric authorization. Disclosure authorization is rechecked after a successful dispatch; changing the authorized workspace rejects the previously stamped path without another POST.

`packages/server/test/personal-account-runtime.test.ts` covers the existing role runtime selecting packaged Free in Free Only/Free Preferred, selecting explicitly authorized qualified personal capacity in Paid Preferred, host-resolved allowances without an explicit request roster, excluding Manual accounts from automatic routing while permitting explicit account selection, measured Auto Best selection and consent withdrawal, safe Free-to-personal continuation after an injected upstream outage, preservation of a successful source edit and its tool observation, and zero paid fallback when Free Only is active. Local disclosure denial produces zero POSTs, zero spending attempts, zero provider failures and zero retries. A role run completing in these fixtures does not satisfy the parent completion gate, independent review or ForgeVerify acceptance gates.

Earlier failing receipts are retained. They exposed connection-alias versus canonical pool identity rejection, stale larger windows on an earlier alias, dropped upstream restriction windows, independent local reservations against a shared upstream account, and rejection of a reservation by its own in-flight classification. Source review also identified that model execution flattened `PersonalAccountPolicyError` into a generic provider error before the role runtime could classify it. The adapter now preserves that error; the focused runtime receipt verifies that local policy rejection cannot poison provider health.

## Authorized live observation

[`openrouter-connected-account-metadata-1791559118027.json`](openrouter-connected-account-metadata-1791559118027.json) records one existing authorized external OpenRouter account. The production connection implementation accepted its valid paid classification. Only two metadata GET requests ran (`/models` and `/key`), with zero inference requests, zero paid requests, zero accounts created and zero models qualified. Paid spending remained off, Free-only credential admission remained unavailable, and no secrets appeared in the public connection view. Restart metadata and local disconnection were checked using headless fixture storage; these observations do not certify desktop encrypted-storage persistence or a GitHub-authenticated session.

The receipt includes source hashes and a worktree-pinned dependency resolution statement. No additional live requests were necessary for the paid-account admission check. There is no live proof of two external accounts, unpaid OAuth entitlement, paid inference, or revocation at the upstream provider. Those acceptance tests remain blocked by missing independent authorized accounts or explicit spending authorization.

The local conservative spending ledger is an application admission control. It does not certify a hard upstream cap or eliminate the possibility of provider-side consumption exceeding an estimate. Main/UI disclosure explicitly states that local budgets cannot guarantee a provider invoice cap. The paid guard therefore requires explicit acknowledgement of that limitation, current authoritative token-only pricing, a positive per-account budget, and both global and per-account spending permission. Automatic top-up remains disabled. The desktop qualification action currently uses eligible unpaid Free capacity; a paid model without existing current qualification has no budget-authorized paid qualification action in settings and remains unavailable. Full parent personal-roster crash resume and a visible per-request paid-cost preview are also not implemented; worker hints and conservative runtime reservation do not substitute for those features.
