# R73 personal account routing addendum

Assessment date: 2026-10-10. This addendum describes the explicitly requested personal account infrastructure. It does not add a live paid inference proof or turn a paid account into packaged Free supply. No account was created, no credential was extracted, and no paid request was executed in this workstream.

## Provider capability and ownership

| Provider | Supported authorization | Authoritative quota owner | Billing control and limitation | R73 eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OpenRouter | Official OAuth PKCE; owner-encrypted credential reference | Authoritative account, workspace or organization metadata; additional keys are not independent accounts | Provider key/workspace/member spend controls exist; current account and exact model prices remain required | Fresh unpaid certificate and exact zero price for Free. Explicit personal paid consent, connection permission, current token-only pricing and durable budget required for personal paid routing |
| Groq | Official API key; no documented account OAuth flow was established | Organization; projects and keys share organization ceilings | Organization spend limits may lag and requests already in progress can finish | Connection/catalog observation and existing key transport; unknown pricing or entitlement blocks automatic execution |
| Other providers | Existing authorized optional connections remain | Must be established separately | No billing guarantee is inferred from a zero token price or a wallet balance | No new personal runtime admission claimed here |

OpenRouter documents browser PKCE authorization and account/workspace association. Current-key metadata exposes creator, organization, workspace, limits and usage; the physical account/organization determines shared capacity, while a CodeForge connection identifies a local permission and budget. The limits documentation explicitly says extra accounts and keys do not multiply globally governed rate limits. Its terms also restrict one individual creating multiple accounts: an application supporting many connections does not authorize prohibited account creation. Legitimate organizational ownership must be distinguished from synthetic accounts. [OAuth](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/auth/oauth), [current-key metadata](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api/api-reference/api-keys/get-current-api-key), [limits](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api_reference/limits), [terms](https://openrouter.ai/terms), [spend controls](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/best-practices/spend-controls).

Groq documents API-key security, projects, organization-scoped rate limits and organization spend limits. It does not establish an OAuth entitlement flow in these materials. Keys or project IDs cannot be treated as separate organization quota domains. Spend controls are not an instantaneous hard stop: the documented delay and in-flight completion make an application estimate a conservative local control rather than a provider-enforced maximum invoice. Authorized customer application use remains subject to the services agreement. [API-key security](https://console.groq.com/docs/production-readiness/security-onboarding), [projects](https://console.groq.com/docs/projects), [rate limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits), [spend limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/spend-limits), [services agreement](https://console.groq.com/docs/legal/services-agreement).

## Runtime policy

The R55 owner-scoped registry is extended, with no second provider router. Legacy explicitly selected CUSTOM sources retain their prior behavior. Typed connections carry a stable `connectionId`, canonical provider, credential reference and exact provider/model qualification receipt. Account metadata cannot qualify a model: the production role helper must yield QUALIFIED for the claimed role, current Reviewer protocol is required, and future or expired evidence is excluded. TESTER and LEAD are not inferred from unrelated qualifications. Source aliases do not change physical quota ownership.

Default policy is FREE_ONLY with paid spending disabled. A fresh authoritative unpaid certificate, terms/price/privacy evidence, exact current zero token prices, no additional billable fees and verified quota ownership are required for personal Free admission. A paid-tier account with a zero-price catalog row remains a paid-authorized personal route; it does not enter the automatic Free pool. Unknown account, pricing or cost scope fails closed unless the separately authorized personal paid policy satisfies all required controls; unknown quota ownership is grouped conservatively across the provider.

The existing role runtime implements Free Preferred, Paid Preferred, Specific Account and Auto Best through the same resolved allowance/fabric path. Free Preferred tries eligible Free before explicitly authorized personal paid fallback; Free Only never authorizes that fallback. Paid Preferred can select an eligible qualified personal account first. Specific Account narrows selection to the owner-selected connection. Manual accounts remain excluded from automatic routing unless explicitly selected. A stored preference does not override current role, owner, privacy, health, quota or spending admission.

Auto Best compares the current measured **role score**, not account balance, a paid label or another role's score. Eligible personal paid routes are ordered by their exact role receipt score; a paid candidate outranks eligible Free only if its measured score is strictly higher. Free ties retain Free. An eligible Free candidate lacking a comparable score is conservatively treated as the maximum for this comparison, avoiding a paid upgrade merely because metadata is missing. Normal FreeFabric selection still applies to the chosen Free pool. This is bounded evidence-based selection, not a claim of universal model-quality ranking.

Both ordinary workflow runs and subagent role execution receive the same trusted host `personalRoleAllowance` callback. It resolves the current owner/connection policy for the actual authorized workspace rather than relying on a separate UI-only router. The parent registers its authorized target workspace and stamps it into isolated Coder/Reviewer requests and durable worker routing hints. Tools remain confined to the child worktree; the target stamp only carries inference privacy/authorization context. Dispatch checks the current host workspace/owner again, and unscoped recovery cannot invent a parent authorization. Connection/request/workspace metadata reaches the trusted guard but is excluded from the upstream request body.

Personal paid execution additionally requires explicit global consent, acknowledged provider billing limitations, connection permission, a finite monthly connection budget, current authoritative USD token-only prices, matching source prices and a bounded `maxTokens`. The trusted host checks owner, connection, provider, credential reference, fingerprint, account freshness and policy again immediately before the physical request. Alternate unpriced models are prohibited within that request.

The durable ledger reserves an upper estimate before dispatch. Its estimate uses serialized UTF-8 prompt bytes with framing overhead and the bounded output maximum at full token prices. This is not a provider invoice guarantee. Verified successful usage releases unused reservation; uncertain failures after dispatch retain the full estimate as consumed. Provider-reported charges cannot lower the charge below measured tokens at authoritative prices. An observed charge exceeding the reservation is retained honestly, output is blocked, and subsequent requests for that connection's monthly budget are blocked, including after restart. When reported USD cannot be represented safely in the numeric ledger, the record retains reported USD and `accountingUncertain`, with a conservative blocked numeric floor rather than an exact invoice total. A certified Free response reporting a charge is therefore not silently accepted. Main/UI disclosure explicitly distinguishes local budgets from provider invoice caps.

Physical account/organization domains serialize in-flight requests and share structured 429 cooldowns across connections. Unknown scope uses one conservative provider domain, never a key fingerprint or invented independent identity. Connection budgets remain separate permissions. Unique physical attempt IDs are persisted before dispatch; replaying one cannot issue a second request. An unreconciled crash hold remains blocked rather than being automatically released as though the provider incurred no cost.

Tool events are held until the exact served model, complete stream and accounting settlement are validated. Substitution, missing identity, truncated tool output, failed accounting and cancellation cannot release buffered tool actions. Parent completion still requires the existing review, ForgeVerify and completion gate.

Local account-policy errors are terminal admission failures in model execution and role runtime. They preserve their typed identity, do not rotate into an unauthorized route, and do not record an upstream provider failure or poison unrelated model/provider health. A budget/owner/price/credential/authorization rejection before the wire is zero provider requests, not a provider outage. Genuine provider authentication rejection is separately classified: automatic personal paid routing can choose another currently authorized qualified personal paid connection, while explicit Specific Account selection stays pinned and blocks without substitution. This does not bypass a shared quota wall or authorize paid fallback in FreeOnly. Other genuine provider errors remain normalized and scoped by the existing health/quota path.

## Current implementation and live limits

**Paid-model role qualification is implemented and fixture-tested, but not live-verified.** The desktop exposes exact-model qualification for eligible paid accounts. It runs bounded Explorer/Reviewer qualification, classifies the receipt as paid, preserves existing Free qualification history, and uses the same guard for explicit permission, estimates, reservation and settlement. A real paid qualification was not run because no spending authorization/budget was provided for this task.

**Parent personal-roster recovery is implemented and deterministic process-fixture tested.** It persists non-secret owner and roster-policy identity, re-resolves current authority/workspace/privacy, preserves edits and validation state, parks absent authority without requests, and recovers only after current authorization is restored. The retained 13/13 real-process scripted-provider receipt is not a live provider or paid execution proof.

**Visible per-request paid-cost preview is implemented and fixture-tested.** Before paid wire dispatch, a visible trusted desktop surface receives a masked account/model and current estimate, waits for renderer paint acknowledgment, and rechecks authority. Hidden, expired or stale presentation fails closed. Local estimates, even when acknowledged, are conservative application controls rather than provider-enforced invoice caps.

## Live read-only account authentication

`openrouter-connected-account-metadata-1791559118027.json` records a genuine existing environment-owned OpenRouter credential through the current `ConnectedAccounts` implementation. Two official GETs (`/api/v1/models` and `/api/v1/key`) returned HTTP 200. The provider reported PAID with verified USER_ACCOUNT scope; the new connection was accepted while FreeOnly credential access and personal paid credential access were denied. Paid spending and dispatch remained off. The catalog contained 469 observed model rows; none was qualified or admitted by this observation.

The harness held credentials and settings only in memory. A fresh local module instance preserved the connection ID and required account recheck before credential dispatch; local disconnect deleted the in-memory secret and withdrew credential access. This is headless host behavior under a clearly labelled fixture local owner, not an actual desktop sign-in, encrypted storage restart or packaged account certification. Source and private compiled-module hashes are recorded; every internal package import was pinned to the R73 worktree. No raw provider account ID, key, credential fingerprint, response body or purchased balance was published. Inference requests, paid requests and created accounts were all zero, and one actual external account was observed.

The first restricted-network run (`openrouter-connected-account-metadata-1791559090559.json`) failed before catalog response. It is retained as a local transport limitation and is not an organic provider outage or a failed credential. A single network-enabled rerun produced the successful receipt. The reproducible bounded script is `benchmarks/r73/observe-connected-openrouter.mjs`; its syntax check passed. It allows only these two read-only URLs and does not use browser/session credential extraction.

FreeFabric projection still requires its own authoritative privacy/training and third-party usage evidence, quota windows and normal admission checks. Projection alone is not a passed Free gate. Unavailable account entitlements and missing qualification are visible blockers rather than invented supply. Hosted remote client-direct execution remains the existing Kilo-specific protocol; the newly measured BlockRun route is supported through installed local desktop/CLI runtime, without a hosted remote route claim.
